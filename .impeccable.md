## Design Context

### Users

People supervising an AI agent while it operates their existing Safari session.
They need an immediate, trustworthy signal showing which tab the agent is
currently controlling without interrupting the page or obscuring its content.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and transparent. The interface should make automation visible
without feeling alarming, playful, or visually noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a confident perimeter glow inspired by the supplied reference: saturated
warm yellow-green light, a crisp edge, and a bright white inner falloff. The
effect belongs around the viewport edge, contains no text, and remains
unmistakable over arbitrary light or dark websites. Motion is a clear breathing
change in opacity, never a flashing or layout-moving animation.

### Design Principles

1. Make AI control unmistakable without blocking page interaction.
2. Keep the effect page-agnostic, lightweight, and outside document layout.
3. Show the indicator only while control is active and clean it up reliably.
4. Respect reduced-motion preferences with a static equivalent.
5. Never alter, capture, or depend on the website's own visual system.
